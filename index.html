Statutory compliance

Every Indian labour law.
Covered.

Hivework automates computation and filing for all central and state requirements — so you never miss a deadline or receive a notice.

PF / EPF
Employees' Provident Fund
ECR challan generation, UAN activation, KYC update. Monthly by the 15th — ready for EPFO portal upload.
ESI
Employee State Insurance
IP number registration, monthly contribution challan for employees with gross ≤ ₹21,000.
PT
Professional Tax
State-wise slab-based deduction and monthly remittance challan — configurable per state.
TDS
Income Tax / TDS
Old and new tax regime, quarterly Form 24Q in NSDL FVU format, annual Form 16 generation.
LWF
Labour Welfare Fund
Biannual state LWF contribution remittance — January and July deadlines tracked automatically.
Factories Act
Factories Act Registers
Auto-generate Form 14 (leave register) and Form 25 (muster roll) in the prescribed format.
Gratuity
Payment of Gratuity Act
Track 5-year eligibility, compute on separation, and maintain gratuity liability provisioning reports.
Bonus
Payment of Bonus Act
Annual statutory bonus at 8.33%–20% of wages within 8 months of financial year end.
Min Wages
Minimum Wages Act
Automated flag when any employee's net wages fall below the state notified minimum wage.
